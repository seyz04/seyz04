## Projects

- **[Discrete Math Practice](https://github.com/seyz04/discrete-math-practice)** · Swift: 24 levels of generated discrete math problems, from logic to recurrences, with every step worked out.
- **[Pregame DJ](https://github.com/seyz04/pregame-dj)** · Python, numpy: an auto-DJ for Apple Music that plans a night along an energy curve and mixes songs with a live audio engine (beat and tempo detection, filters, beat-synced echo).
